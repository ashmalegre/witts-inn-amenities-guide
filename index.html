Play Music with Sonos

  1. Connect your phone to the house Wi‑Fi above.
  2. Download or open the Sonos app. Sign in or create an account only if prompted.
  3. The app should detect the available speakers. If you see an Add prompt and the speaker is flashing green, tap Add and follow the on-screen prompts.
  4. Pick the room you want to use.
  5. Select your music and press play.
iOS · App Store Android · Google Play

Using AirPlay on iPhone / iPad

  1. Make sure your device is connected to Witt’s Inn Wi‑Fi.
  2. Open Control Center and tap the AirPlay / audio-output icon.
  3. Select the available Sonos speaker, then press play.

Outdoor TV Audio

  1. Turn on the outdoor TV.
  2. Open the TV’s Sound / Audio settings and select the connected speakers as the audio output.
Please do not reset or reconfigure the speakers.
Pool & Spa

Using the Pool & Spa

Witt’s Inn uses a Hayward pool & spa system. The spa is preset to 99°F and programmed for an approximately one-hour cycle.

99°FSpa temp
~15 minWarm-up
1 hourHeat cycle

To Heat the Spa

  1. On the Hayward pool / spa control, select Spa.
  2. The spa is preset to 99°F — no temperature adjustment is needed.
  3. Allow about 15 minutes for the spa to warm.
  4. Once the cycle starts, leave the other pool / spa mode settings as they are.

Jets & Water Features

  1. The spa’s blower and jets typically activate automatically as the spa heats.
  2. Separate controls are also available to turn the blower or jets on manually, if desired.

After One Hour

  1. The spa heating cycle ends automatically after approximately one hour.
  2. The system then returns to Pool / Spillover mode, allowing warm spa water to flow into the pool.
  3. The pool may feel warmer afterward — this is expected.
A quick note

Please leave the system settings as programmed and avoid repeatedly switching between Pool and Spa modes.
